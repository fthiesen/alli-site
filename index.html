My approach

Therapy that adapts to you

Some people process emotions best through conversation. Others think through movement, rhythm, images, or creative experiences.

  • Some need silence.
  • Some need play.
  • Some need structure.
  • Some simply need someone who understands.

As a Registered Psychotherapist with specialized training in Music Psychotherapy and Expressive Arts Therapy, I adapt each session to fit the person—not the other way around.

For some clients, sessions look much like traditional talk therapy. For others, music or creative experiences provide another pathway into healing. You never need musical ability, artistic skill, or previous experience.

Together, we'll discover what works best for you.

More about my approach

There is no “right” way to do therapy.

  • Some sessions are entirely conversation.
  • Some include music.
  • Some include creative activities.
  • Some involve mindfulness or movement.
  • Many combine several approaches.

Every session is guided by your goals, comfort, and needs.
