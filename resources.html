Resources

Resources, reflections, and information to support your journey.

Therapy often begins before the first appointment. Sometimes it begins with a question:

  • “Why is my child struggling?”
  • “Why don't traditional approaches feel like a fit?”
  • “How can I better understand myself or someone I love?”

This space offers reflections and resources on mental health, neurodiversity, creativity, grief, and the many ways people experience and navigate the world.
