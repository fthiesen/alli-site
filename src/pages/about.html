About

Therapy should fit the person—not the other way around.

Seeking support can feel vulnerable. Whether you're reaching out for yourself, your child, or your family, finding a therapist who truly understands your needs can make all the difference.

My goal is to create a space where people feel safe, respected, and free to show up exactly as they are. Every person experiences the world differently, and I believe therapy should reflect that.

There is no single "right" way to heal. Together, we'll discover an approach that feels meaningful and works for you.

Meet Alli

I've always been drawn to the ways people express themselves beyond words.

Over the years, my work has shown me that healing doesn't always happen through conversation alone. Sometimes people need space to create, move, reflect, laugh, sit quietly, or simply be understood.

As a Registered Psychotherapist with specialized training in Music Psychotherapy and additional training in Expressive Arts Therapy, I bring a flexible, client-centred approach to every session. Some clients prefer traditional talk therapy, while others benefit from incorporating music, creative expression, mindfulness, or experiential approaches.

There is no expectation that you have any musical or artistic experience. These are simply tools that may be available if they support your goals.

Each session is shaped around what feels most helpful for you.

What working together looks like

Therapy is adapted to you, not the other way around.

  • Some sessions look very much like traditional psychotherapy and are entirely conversation-based.
  • Some include music, art, mindfulness, movement, or other creative experiences.
  • Many combine several approaches over time.

The focus is never on making music or creating art—it is on helping you move toward your goals in a way that feels natural, comfortable, and meaningful.

My approach

What guides my work

01

Neurodiversity-affirming

I recognize and respect the many ways people think, communicate, learn, and experience the world. Therapy builds on strengths rather than trying to "fix" differences.

02

Collaborative

You are the expert on your own life. Together we'll explore what is working, what isn't, and what meaningful change looks like for you.

03

Flexible

Every client is different. Sessions evolve with your needs and may include conversation, creative expression, mindfulness, music, or other therapeutic approaches.

04

Inclusive

I strive to create a welcoming, affirming space for people of all identities and backgrounds, including members of the 2SLGBTQIA+ community.
