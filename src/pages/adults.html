How I work

A personalized approach to therapy

Therapy is not one-size-fits-all. I draw from a variety of evidence-informed therapeutic approaches and tailor sessions to your individual needs, goals, and experiences.

For many adults, therapy involves meaningful conversation, reflection, and developing a deeper understanding of thoughts, emotions, patterns, and relationships. Together, we may explore strategies for managing anxiety, navigating difficult emotions, building self-compassion, strengthening coping skills, and creating meaningful change.

My approach may incorporate a range of therapeutic methods depending on what is most helpful for you. This may include cognitive and behavioural approaches, emotion regulation strategies, exploring different parts of yourself, and developing greater awareness of your experiences.

For some individuals, creative approaches such as music, mindfulness, or expressive practices may provide additional ways to explore emotions or experiences that are difficult to put into words. These approaches are always optional and guided by your comfort and preferences.

The focus is always on creating a therapeutic process that feels relevant, supportive, and meaningful for you.

You don't have to wait until things feel “bad enough.”

Many people seek therapy not because something is "wrong," but because they want to understand themselves better, strengthen relationships, navigate change, or create a life that feels more aligned.

Therapy can be a space for growth, reflection, healing, and change.
