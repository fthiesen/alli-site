Who I help

Whatever brings you here, you don't have to figure it out alone.

People seek therapy for many different reasons. Some are navigating a new diagnosis. Others are feeling overwhelmed, grieving a loss, struggling with anxiety, or simply looking for a different way forward.

Whether you're seeking support for yourself or someone you love, therapy can provide a space to better understand what's happening and develop tools that support meaningful change.
