Let's connect.

Choosing a therapist is a personal decision. Finding someone who feels like the right fit matters.

Whether you are looking for support for yourself, your child, your teen, or your family, I welcome you to reach out. A consultation is an opportunity to share what brings you here, ask questions, and determine whether my approach may be a good fit for your needs.

Start with a consultation

I offer a free initial consultation to discuss:

  • What you are looking for support with
  • Your goals for therapy
  • Whether my approach aligns with your needs
  • Questions about fees or the therapy process

You do not need to know exactly what kind of therapy you need before reaching out. We can explore that together.
